Retail campaigns that return 3x their ad spend

Seasonal and collection-launch campaigns for fashion and retail brands that returned more than three times their ad spend.

By Muhammad Awais Rustam · 14 Oct 2026

3x+return on ad spend
+20%conversion rate

The challenge

Fashion and jewellery brands needed limited-edition collections to sell out quickly, and a Qatar hypermarket needed footfall around Ramadan, Eid and National Day.

What I did

  1. Ran Facebook and Instagram campaigns for each seasonal and limited-edition collection.
  2. Used remarketing and product-focused content to bring back people who had already shown interest.
  3. Planned daily and seasonal campaign calendars for Habari Hypermarket.
  4. Ran hyperlocal ads to bring nearby customers into stores.

The outcome

Collection launches consistently returned more than three times their ad spend, and remarketing lifted conversion rates by 20%.
