About

Strategy, media buying and design in one person.

I’m Muhammad Awais Rustam, a Digital Marketing Specialist and Graphic Designer based in Doha, Qatar. For more than seven years I’ve helped brands grow through paid social, search, SEO, email and WhatsApp marketing, always measured against leads and revenue rather than likes.

Today I lead digital marketing and design for Target Group, a Qatari group of three industrial companies: Target Metal Forming, Target Furniture Factory and Target Trading & Contracting. My focus there is B2B: contractors, fit-out firms and designers who buy stainless steel, wood panels and hardware. I generate more than 200 qualified B2B leads a month and work them through to sales.

Because I design my own creatives, campaigns move fast. The idea, the visual, the targeting and the reporting all come from the same desk.
