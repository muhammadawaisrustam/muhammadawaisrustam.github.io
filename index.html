About me

Who is Muhammad Awais Rustam?

Muhammad Awais Rustam is a digital marketing specialist based in Doha, Qatar, with more than seven years of experience in B2B lead generation, performance marketing, search engine marketing (SEM), SEO and social media marketing. He currently leads digital marketing for Target Group, where he generates more than 200 qualified B2B leads every month.

My work is measured in leads, cost per lead and revenue. I plan the strategy, set up and optimise Meta Ads and Google Ads campaigns, build content calendars, and follow leads through WhatsApp and email until they reach the sales team.
